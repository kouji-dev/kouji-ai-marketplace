Colors

Every colour is a token in tokens.css. Components and pages use the tokens, never a raw value. Values below are read live from the page, so they can't drift.

Surfaces

SwatchTokenUseValue
--bgPage background
--panelCards, panels
--panel-2Raised or nested surfaces
--lineBorders and dividers

Ink

SwatchTokenUseValue
--inkPrimary text
--ink-2Secondary text
--ink-3Labels, hints

Accent and states

SwatchTokenUseValue
--accentPrimary actions, focus
--accent-inkAccent text and links
--okSuccess
--dangerErrors, destructive actions
